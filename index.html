Dumpster Rental Agreement & Payment Authorization

This agreement is between Dumpster Crawl & Dumpster Rentals and the customer identified in the booking. By checking the box and electronically signing, the customer confirms that the booking information is accurate and agrees to these terms.

Rental period and pickup

  • All packages include 10 days.
  • Pickup is automatically scheduled 10 days after delivery.
  • Call or text 262-664-9198 for early pickup.
  • Extra days and trips may result in additional charges.

Delivery, placement, and access

  • Provide accurate placement instructions and keep the delivery area clear and accessible.
  • If delivery cannot be completed, reasonable contact attempts will be made. A $49 failed-delivery fee may be charged before a refund.
  • The dumpster may not be moved without written permission.
  • Blocked or unsafe pickup access may result in additional trip, delay, towing, or recovery charges.

Loading and prohibited materials

  • Material must remain at or below the fill line. A $125 overfill fee may apply.
  • Excess weight is charged at $69 per ton, prorated when applicable.
  • No appliances, tires, batteries, paint, chemicals, hazardous waste, concrete, dirt, or bulk lumber without written approval.
  • The customer is responsible for costs, fines, cleanup, rejected loads, or extra trips caused by prohibited or improperly loaded materials.

Property and responsibility

  • The customer accepts responsibility for the placement location and required permits.
  • The customer is responsible for loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized movement, or damage while the dumpster is at the rental address.
  • Driveway protection reduces risk but does not guarantee surfaces, lawns, curbs, utilities, or underground structures will not be damaged.
  • The customer agrees to indemnify Dumpster Crawl & Dumpster Rentals for claims arising from the customer’s placement choice, use, loading, or prohibited materials, except where prohibited by law.

Payment authorization

  • New rentals begin unpaid until payment is completed.
  • A disclosed 3% processing fee applies to card payments.
  • The customer authorizes a secure payment processor to keep a payment method on file and authorizes documented charges for excess weight, extra days, prohibited materials, damage, failed delivery, extra trips, towing, recovery, or other charges allowed by this agreement.
  • Card numbers are stored by the payment processor, never this booking application.

Electronic consent

The checked acceptance, typed legal name, booking timestamp, and submitted photo ID together constitute electronic acceptance.
